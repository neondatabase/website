Import `drizzleParsers` from `@neon/realtime-drizzle/client`. It runs in the browser: spread it into the client's `parsers` option so dates and timestamps decode the way Drizzle decodes them. The [Drizzle example](/docs/realtime/examples/drizzle) shows it in use.
