Import the TanStack DB adapter from `@neon/realtime-tanstack`. It runs in the browser, where [`realtimeCollectionOptions`](#tanstack-db-realtimecollectionoptions) turns a sealed query into a [TanStack DB collection](https://tanstack.com/db/latest/docs/overview#defining-collections). It's taught in the [TanStack DB section](/docs/realtime/guides/reactivity#tanstack-db) of the reactivity guide. The mutations guide covers [optimistic writes](/docs/realtime/guides/mutations#with-tanstack-db-collections), and the SSR guide covers [hydration](/docs/realtime/guides/ssr#with-tanstack-db).
