## More with Lakebase Search

[Lakebase Search](/docs/ai/lakebase-search) adds scalable vector, keyword, and hybrid search to Postgres through the `lakebase_vector` and `lakebase_text` extensions. This week we have two new resources to help you put it to work:

- **Case study:** [CommSync runs text, vector, and hybrid search on Postgres with Lakebase Search](/blog/commsync-runs-text-vector-and-hybrid-search-on-postgres-with-lakebase-search). See how a unified business inbox powers its main inbox (BM25 text search), AI assistant (semantic search), and command palette (hybrid search with Reciprocal Rank Fusion) from a single database, cutting query latency from 19.5 seconds to 18.6 milliseconds.
- **Guide:** [Build image search over CLIP embeddings with Lakebase Search](/guides/clip-image-search). Search a Flickr30k corpus by text, by image, and by caption from one `vector(512)` column, using the `lakebase_ann` index (IVF + RaBitQ) that builds 50 to 100 times faster than HNSW.

## Per-project permissions

Neon now has per-project permissions, giving agents and developers exactly the right level of access on individual projects. Access works in two layers that combine additively, so a grant can only raise a user's access on a project, never lower it:

- **Organization roles** set a baseline across every project: **Admin** (full control), **Editor** (work in every project, but can't delete or transfer them), **Viewer** (read-only), and the new **Collaborator** role (no access until granted specific projects).
- **Per-project permissions** grant extra access on individual projects: **Viewer** (read-only), **Editor** (connect, query, and edit resources), and **Admin** (manage access, settings, and the project lifecycle).

Grant per-project permissions from a project's **Settings** &rarr; **Project permissions** page. Everyone keeps the access they have today: Admins stay Admins, Members become Editors, project creators become Admin on projects they created, and existing project-share access becomes Editor access. The new Collaborator role plus per-project permissions replaces project sharing, which is being deprecated.

To learn more, see [Per-project permissions](/docs/manage/user-permissions#per-project-permissions).
